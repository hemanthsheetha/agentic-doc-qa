# On-call Handbook

## 1. Rotations

Each Tier 1 service has a weekly rotation that starts on Monday at 10:00 Eastern. Every rotation has a primary and a secondary engineer. Nobody is on call more than one week in every four.

## 2. Escalation contacts

The Engineering Manager on duty is the third escalation step for SEV1 incidents; the manager-on-duty schedule is published in PagerDuty as "EM-on-duty". For data-loss incidents, also page the Data Platform lead, Priya Raman. For security incidents, page the security on-call through the "sec-oncall" PagerDuty service.

## 3. Compensation

Engineers receive an on-call stipend of $350 for each week as primary and $175 for each week as secondary. Time spent actively working a page outside 09:00-18:00 local time is paid as overtime under the Compensation Policy §3.

## 4. Handoff

At the end of each rotation, the outgoing primary writes a handoff note covering open incidents, flaky alerts and pending postmortem action items (see the Incident Response Runbook §6).

## 5. Recovery time

After a night with more than 2 hours of active paging, the engineer may start work up to 4 hours late the next day without using leave.
