# Privacy Incident Procedure

## 1. What counts as a privacy incident

Any unauthorized access to, or loss of, personal data, including driver GPS traces from the Driver App and customer contact details.

## 2. Notification deadlines

The Privacy Officer must be told within 4 hours of discovery. When the incident is likely to harm individuals, the Privacy Officer notifies the relevant regulator within 72 hours of discovery. Affected customers are notified without undue delay, and no later than 5 business days after discovery.

## 3. Incident handling

Confirmed loss of customer data is a SEV1. Because it is a data-loss incident, the Data Platform lead is paged in addition to the usual escalation path. Customer-facing messages about a privacy incident must be approved by the Privacy Officer before they are sent.

## 4. Evidence preservation

Logs and records related to the incident are placed under a legal hold, so they are kept beyond their normal retention period until the hold is lifted.
