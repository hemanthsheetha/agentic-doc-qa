# Data Retention Policy

## 1. Principles

We keep personal data only as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected for.

## 2. Retention periods

Shipment records: 7 years, for customs and tax audits. Customer invoices: 7 years. Driver GPS traces: 90 days, after which they are aggregated. Support tickets: 3 years after closure. Application logs: 30 days. Backups that contain these records follow the Backup and Recovery Standard §3, but restored data must be re-purged to these periods within 30 days.

## 3. Deletion requests

Customer deletion requests are completed within 30 days. Records under a legal hold are exempt until the hold is lifted.
