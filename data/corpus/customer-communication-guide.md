# Customer Communication Guide

## 1. Channels

Incident updates are posted on the status page and emailed to the customer's registered technical contacts.

## 2. Tone

Updates are factual, avoid speculating about root cause, and never name individual employees.

## 3. Update cadence by severity

SEV1: first update within 30 minutes of declaration, then every 30 minutes until resolved. SEV2: first update within 1 hour, then every 2 hours. SEV3: a single update once a workaround is available. SEV4: no proactive update. For SEV1 and SEV2, a final resolution notice goes out within 24 hours of resolution, and a customer-facing postmortem summary within 10 business days.
