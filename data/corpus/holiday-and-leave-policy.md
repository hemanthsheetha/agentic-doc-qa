# Holiday and Leave Policy

## 1. Statutory holidays

We observe New Year's Day, Good Friday, Victoria Day, Canada Day, the Civic Holiday, Labour Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas Day and Boxing Day.

## 2. Vacation

Employees receive 20 days of paid vacation per year, rising to 25 days after 5 years of service.

## 3. On-call over holidays

An engineer who is the primary on-call over a statutory holiday receives one extra day of paid leave, in addition to the regular on-call stipend. Active paging worked on a statutory holiday is paid at the statutory holiday overtime rate in the Compensation Policy.

## 4. Rest after paging

Rest after a heavy night of paging is covered by the On-call Handbook, not by this policy, and does not use vacation days.
