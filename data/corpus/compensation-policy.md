# Compensation Policy

## 1. Pay schedule

Salaries are paid every second Friday by direct deposit.

## 2. Bonuses

The annual performance bonus target is 10% of base salary for individual contributors and 15% for managers. Bonuses are paid in March.

## 3. Overtime

Eligible overtime is paid at 1.5 times the employee's regular hourly rate. Overtime worked between 00:00 and 06:00 local time, or on a statutory holiday, is paid at 2 times the regular hourly rate. Overtime must be logged in Workday within 7 days.

## 4. Expense reimbursement

Approved expense claims are reimbursed with the next pay run. Eligibility rules are in the Travel and Expense Policy §3.
