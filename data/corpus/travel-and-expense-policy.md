# Travel and Expense Policy

## 1. Scope

Applies to all employees and contractors travelling on company business.

## 2. Booking

Flights and hotels must be booked through Navan at least 14 days in advance. Exceptions are travel for client-site incidents (see the Incident Response Runbook §4) and travel approved by a VP. Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is allowed on longer flights.

## 3. Expenses and per diem

The meal per diem is $75 CAD per day within Canada and $90 CAD per day elsewhere. Hotel caps are $250 CAD per night in Toronto, Montreal and Vancouver, and $180 CAD per night elsewhere in Canada. Receipts are required for any single expense above $25. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the trip.

## 4. Approvals

The employee's manager approves trips under $2,000 CAD. Trips of $2,000 CAD or more also need a director's approval.
